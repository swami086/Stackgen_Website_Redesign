[StackGen ObserveNow](https://console.opsverse.io/auth/login) allows you to launch your instances of `ObserveNow`, `DeployNow`, and `ONE` on StackGen's own cloud.

In these cases, you don't have to worry about bringing up any infrastructure - the products are installed on the following pre-provisioned clusters:

- Shared: the most cost-effective, running alongside other logically separated tenants
- Dedicated: an option for more catered workloads

This option is available on all regions of AWS, GCP and AZ.
