Aiden is a DevOps Copilot designed to support you across the entire DevOps lifecycle. You can interact with Aiden just like you would with a teammate on your DevOps team.

- Need to configure a CI/CD pipeline? Aiden can do it.
- Troubleshooting a production outage? Aiden can help.
- Setting up IAM policies for engineers? Aiden has you covered.

Behind the scenes, Aiden uses a multi-modal, LLM-powered architecture composed of specialized agents. These agents work together to perform context-aware DevOps tasks with speed and accuracy.

![home screen](https://docs.stackgen.com/assets/images/aiden-dee1811dffc0781253a6e110826f4c66.png)

## How Aiden Works?

Aiden is built on an agentic model:

- **Agents for specialized tasks**: Each agent is designed for a specific DevOps function.
- **Configurable granularity**: You can control how detailed or broad Aiden’s actions should be.
- **Scalable across teams**: Different teams can customize Aiden to meet their own requirements and constraints.

Adding a new tool is simple—just add a new agent. Each new agent expands Aiden’s capabilities, making it more effective at handling complex DevOps workflows.

Licensing and Access

Aiden is available under two license types:

- **Aiden User:** You can access Aiden via [https://aiden.stackgen.com](https://aiden.stackgen.com/) for a focused AI experience. This standalone license is ideal for teams who want to get started quickly with Aiden's AI capabilities. You have the flexibility to [Deploy Aiden](/docs/aiden/1.0/deployment/deploymentoptions) in multiple ways.

- **StackGen Platform User:** For a more integrated experience, you can access Aiden as part of your StackGen platform via your company-specific URL. For example, `https://yourcompany.stackgen.com/ai`. This integrated license provides a targeted, context-aware experience by connecting Aiden with your StackGen projects. This license gives you the flexibility to associate your workspaces with existing StackGen projects, letting Aiden to access your appStacks, governance policies, and project-specific context for more accurate and relevant outputs. This is deployed on the [Cloud](/docs/aiden/1.0/deployment/cloud).
