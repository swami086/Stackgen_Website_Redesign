Configure and manage your Aiden environment, integrations, and team settings to customize your DevOps experience.

- [**Workspaces**\\
\\
Organize and manage your projects and environments with dedicated workspaces for different teams and contexts.](/docs/aiden/1.0/settings/workspaces)
- [**Organisation Users**\\
\\
Manage team members, roles, and permissions to control access and collaboration within your organisation.](/docs/aiden/1.0/settings/orgusers)
- [**Contact Points**\\
\\
Configure notification destinations and alert channels to stay informed about Aiden's activities and system events.](/docs/aiden/1.0/settings/contactpoints)
- [**Remote Runners**\\
Set up agents to connect Aiden to tools and integrations within your private environment without internet exposure.](/docs/aiden/1.0/settings/runners)
- [**API Configuration**\\
\\
Set up API keys, authentication, and integration endpoints to connect Aiden with your external tools and services.](/docs/aiden/1.0/settings/api)
