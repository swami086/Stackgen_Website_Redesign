Alert groups help organize and manage related alerts, improving the efficiency of alert handling.

### Working with Alert Groups

1. Navigate to **Alerting --> Groups**
2. View grouped alerts:
   - See alerts grouped by rule or custom criteria
   - Understand the overall state of your systems
3. Manage group settings:
   - Adjust grouping parameters
   - Customize group notifications
4. Apply bulk actions to grouped alerts:
   - Silence multiple alerts simultaneously
   - Acknowledge or resolve groups of alerts

### Benefits of Alert Groups

- Reduce noise by consolidating related alerts
- Improve context for incident response
- Simplify management of large-scale alert systems
