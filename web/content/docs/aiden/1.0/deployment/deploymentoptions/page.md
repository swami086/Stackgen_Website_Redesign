StackGen Aiden supports **cloud**, **private SaaS**, and **self-hosted** models. Choose the approach that fits your security, data residency, and operations requirements.

## Choose a deployment model

Each option balances who runs the infrastructure, where data lives, and how much your team manages day to day.

- [**Cloud**\\
\\
Fully managed SaaS on StackGen infrastructure. Shared or dedicated clusters, VPC peering, and coverage across major cloud regions.](/docs/aiden/1.0/deployment/cloud)
- [**Private SaaS**\\
\\
Aiden runs in your cloud with StackGen managing the platform remotely. Strong fit for data residency, compliance, and VPC-level control.](/docs/aiden/1.0/deployment/private-saas)
- [**Self-hosted**\\
\\
You operate Aiden inside your environment. For enterprises with strict operational needs; coordinate with customer success to enable this model.](/docs/aiden/1.0/deployment/self-hosted)

Licensing and deployment

Aiden is available under two license types, and deployment options depend on which applies:

- **Aiden user:** You can deploy on [Cloud](/docs/aiden/1.0/deployment/cloud), [Private SaaS](/docs/aiden/1.0/deployment/private-saas/cluster-requirements), or [Self-hosted](/docs/aiden/1.0/deployment/self-hosted).
- **StackGen Platform user:** Aiden is deployed on the [Cloud](/docs/aiden/1.0/deployment/cloud).
