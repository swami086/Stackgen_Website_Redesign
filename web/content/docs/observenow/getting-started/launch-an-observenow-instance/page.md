To launch an `ObserveNow` instance sign in/login to your [StackGen Admin Console](https://console.opsverse.io/) and follow the steps below:

- Under App Catalog, click on `ObserveNow` card.

![](https://docs.stackgen.com/assets/images/ygUvUeJ9c_2kVQ04_zUmZ_screenshot-2024-03-04-at-123602-54b49b55f57ae68037e00b9a86e33265.png)

- If you're signing in for the first time, you will see an overview of `ObserveNow`. Click `LAUNCH NEW`.

![](https://docs.stackgen.com/assets/images/HzSD39qAOQjIIa7JFvJ6B_screenshot-2024-03-04-at-123614-4d6df3689398bbbc20e9e2b59e3e2a85.png)

- Launch new wizard will be launched. Select the region and provide a name for your instance and click `LAUNCH`.

![](https://docs.stackgen.com/assets/images/4468CNTLeXzGBFpd2oQoV_screenshot-2024-03-04-at-124124-bca1e4da3abf0563683a5a2420b71f9c.png)

info

When subscribed to ObserveNow, you can choose any available regions or deploy it on private SaaS.

- Within a minute your ObserveNow stack will be up and running and you should see your ObserveNow instance under `Active Apps`.

![](https://docs.stackgen.com/assets/images/3VI4zHZeNoo4IAnEtyVI4_screenshot-2024-03-04-at-124441-3662cb146250f9ddc1c202ff71210050.png)
