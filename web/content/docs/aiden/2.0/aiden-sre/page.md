Aiden SRE is your AI Site Reliability Engineer (SRE). It works alongside your team to keep services healthy: it discovers the entities across your observability stack, automates alert triage to cut noise, and runs investigations so you can move from signal to resolution faster.

Instead of switching between dashboards and tools, you get one operating model where Aiden does the reliability legwork and surfaces what matters, scoped to your workspace.
