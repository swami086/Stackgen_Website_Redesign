# Script

## Buried in alerts (Frame 2)

    Your on-call team is buried in alerts, and most of them don't matter. The ones that do can take hours to untangle.

## Meet Aiden for SRE (Frame 3)

    Meet Aiden for SRE, your AI SRE teammate. It connects to the observability tools you already run and maps your services on its own.

## Flood of alerts (Frame 5)

    One failure can set off a flood of alerts.

## Aiden triages (Frame 6)

    Aiden triages every alert as it arrives. It groups related alerts, filters the noise, and ranks the rest by impact on your services.

## Only what needs you (Frame 7)

    Your engineers see only the alerts that need them, and save their energy for real incidents.

## Incident hits (Frame 9)

    When a real incident hits, most of the time goes into investigation.

## Investigation (golden) (Frame 10)

    Aiden starts investigating the moment an alert fires. It correlates logs, metrics and events across your dependencies. Then it scores every possible cause against the evidence, and even tries to prove itself wrong.

## Probable root cause (Frame 11)

    Your team starts from a probable root cause, and MTTR comes down.

## Fix is often manual (Frame 13)

    Even with the cause in hand, the fix is often manual.

## Aiden runs the remediation (Frame 14)

    Aiden runs the remediation, from restarting a service or scaling out to rerouting traffic or rolling back a deployment. Your team sets which actions need approval, and every action goes into a full audit trail.

## Incidents close faster (Frame 15)

    Incidents close faster, and your team stays in control of what runs.

## Keeps learning (Frame 16)

    And it keeps learning. Every investigation adds to what Aiden knows about your environment, so the next incident starts further ahead.

## World Model and Aiden OS (Frame 18)

    Aiden for SRE runs on the Aiden World Model, the shared record of what's deployed, what changed, what broke and what fixed it. And Aiden OS holds every action to your policies.

## Call to action (Frame 19)

    See Aiden for SRE on your own alerts. Book a demo, or try the free Community Edition.
