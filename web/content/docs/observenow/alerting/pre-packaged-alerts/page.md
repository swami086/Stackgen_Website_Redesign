StackGen ObserveNow includes 100s pre-packaged alerts that cover the alerting needs for common infra and app systems. These alerts are available out-of-the-box under the `OpsVerse` folder within Grafana.

![](https://docs.stackgen.com/assets/images/A1ybFzEd4Yg88MHjQbQjT-KWLVo_GsYm9rfoogLhq0h-20240802-025234-28e9801610f0cd5d0fe7aaf31135262e.png)

These alert provide a great way to immediately get started with full stack observability. Alerts can be further edited to suit an organizations needs.
