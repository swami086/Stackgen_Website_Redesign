The History page lists your past chats. You can search, filter, favorite, reopen, or delete conversations.

![history](https://docs.stackgen.com/assets/images/history1-4c8480fa2ad3eb13df4646e82aaedff2.png)

Here's what each field represents:

**1\. Title**: Shows the conversation title (usually the first user message).

- In case of **Tasks**, you'll find a stack icon that is clickable, and opens the relevant task definition.



![task definition](https://docs.stackgen.com/assets/images/taskdefinition-536faadd3609333eedb53b05e1ebd4dd.png)


**2\. Interaction Start**: The timestamp of when the conversation began.

- **Sortable**: click the header to sort the list in ascending or descending order.

**3\. Search bar**: You can search for tasks and chats using the search bar.

**4\. History Filter**: History is categorised into [**Tasks**](/docs/aiden/1.0/tasks) and **Chat Conversations**. You can filter the history based on these two filter options.

**5\. Actions**: This column lets you:

- **Star**: mark or unmark a chat or task as **Favorite**.
- **Chat bubble**: reopen the conversation in the chat window.
- **Trash can**: delete the conversation permanently.

**6\. Favorites**: Lets you view all the tasks and chats that are favorited.
