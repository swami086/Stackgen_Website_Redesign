If you're:

- An **Aiden User:** You can access Aiden directly at [https://aiden.stackgen.com](https://aiden.stackgen.com/) for a focused AI experience.
- **A StackGen Platform User:** You can access Aiden via your company-specific URL (e.g., `https://yourcompany.stackgen.com/ai`).

You can access Aiden through different modalities, depending on what your team is most comfortable with. Currently, supported modality is Web Sign-up, however, there are many more that will be coming soon and We've listed them below.

## Web Sign-up

Sign-up for an Aiden account by visitng [https://aiden.stackgen.com/signup](https://aiden.stackgen.com/signup). The Web interface also allows your StackGen Admins to configure integrations, setup skills, add users, and manage Aiden.

## Coming Soon

### Backstage

Our upcoming Backstage plugin will allow you to interact with Aiden directly within your developer portal. When installed, it will create a native Backstage interface for chatting with Aiden without context switching. The plugin will automatically sync with your Backstage software catalog, enabling Aiden to:

- Understand your service architecture and dependencies
- Access API specifications and documentation
- Learn about your teams and ownership structures
- Track service metadata and technical documentation

This deep integration means Aiden will provide more contextual assistance by leveraging your entire Backstage ecosystem, making it an even more effective DevOps companion within your existing workflows.

### MS Teams

Engage with Aiden through Microsoft Teams to streamline your DevOps workflows. Get instant assistance, manage deployments, and collaborate with your team using natural conversations in your Teams channels.

### Browser Plugin

Our upcoming browser plugin will bring Aiden's capabilities directly to your favorite DevOps tools. The plugin will intelligently recognize when you're working in supported platforms like:

- GitHub/GitLab repositories
- Jenkins pipelines
- Grafana
- Kubernetes dashboards
- AWS Console
- Azure Portal
- ArgoCD
- And more.

The extension will be context-aware, understanding exactly what you're viewing or working on in these tools. This means when you activate Aiden, it will already know the relevant repository, pipeline, cluster, or cloud resource you're dealing with, thus enabling more precise and immediate assistance without having to provide additional context.
